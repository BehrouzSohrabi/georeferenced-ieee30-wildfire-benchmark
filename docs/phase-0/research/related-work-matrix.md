### Source-supported differentiators for the proposed georeferenced IEEE 30-bus wildfire benchmark

1. **Explicit wildfire-to-grid co-simulation outputs at a compact teaching/validation scale.**  
   The IEEE Access source applies FARSITE to an IEEE 30-bus system, overlays fire output with grid component coordinates, runs ACOPF after fire-induced modifications, and reports component exposure plus flows, voltages, generator outputs, outages, susceptibility, vulnerability, and risk across **4,116** scenarios. CATS and RTS-GMLC provide grid/GIS assets but no verified distributed wildfire scenario-output corpus.  
   *Source:* [Sohrabi et al. PDF](https://nwfirescience.org/sites/default/files/publications/A_Wildfire_Progression_Simulation_and_Risk-Rating_Methodology_for_Power_Grid_Infrastructure.pdf).

2. **Reproducible, auditable dataset packaging would fill the identified article-to-data gap.**  
   The IEEE Access article verifies methodology/case-study results but no authoritative public deposit of its mapped IEEE-30 coordinates, line geometry, FARSITE input/output files, or per-scenario grid-result tables was verified. A versioned package with those artifacts would therefore be a concrete advance rather than merely another risk-method paper.  
   *Evidence boundary:* this is a verified absence from the authoritative records checked, **not** a claim that no artifact exists anywhere.

3. **Geometry fidelity can be made explicit and inspectable.**  
   RTS-GMLC’s released GIS branch features are direct two-endpoint `LineString`s; ACTIVSg2000’s checked repository has no coordinate columns or GIS geometry. CATS demonstrates a stronger GIS precedent with `LineString`/`MultiLineString` assets and specifically identifies why point-to-point approximations can distort wildfire-risk intersection. The proposed benchmark can state whether each IEEE-30 line is a straight synthetic segment or a supplied polyline—and attach CRS, provenance, and validation rules.  
   *Sources:* [RTS-GMLC branch GeoJSON](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GridMod/RTS-GMLC/master/RTS_Data/FormattedData/GIS/branch.geojson), [CATS GIS lines](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/WISPO-POP/CATS-CaliforniaTestSystem/master/GIS/CATS_lines.json), [CATS paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.04351), [ACTIVSg2000 README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/caseformat/ACTIVSg2000/master/README).
