# Technical scope and evidence standard

## Principal contribution

The v1.0 dataset contribution is a **technically validated, reusable georeferencing of the IEEE 30-bus test system**: 30 synthetic bus locations and 41 synthetic branch corridors represented as an explicit spatial network. It is designed to make a standard electrical topology usable in geospatial method development without representing a real utility system.

Wildfire and grid-impact pipelines demonstrate one class of downstream use. They are neither the ownership claim nor the evidence basis for the spatial dataset's technical validity.

## Required technical evidence before a v1.0 release claim

1. **Topology fidelity:** every released branch has a stable ID, directed `fbus`/`tbus` crosswalk, declared type, and reconciliation evidence against the selected study topology.
2. **Spatial integrity:** all features are valid; declared CRS and units are explicit; branch endpoints coincide with their corresponding bus points within a predeclared metric tolerance; no unintended self-intersections, duplicate IDs, or disconnected/unexplained components occur.
3. **Metric correctness:** lengths and areas used for analysis are computed in California Albers (EPSG:3310), not from longitude/latitude degrees. The 702 km value from the source paper is a comparison target, not a value to force; any difference is disclosed in the discrepancy log.
4. **Reproducibility:** the GeoJSON is regenerated by script from frozen, documented input references; release manifests/checksums and automated tests verify the exact payload.
5. **Interpretability:** the data dictionary states every field, source/derivation, CRS, units, coordinate precision, branch type, and known limitation. The datasheet describes intended use, prohibited operational use, and extension requirements.

## Boundaries

- The spatial placement and routes are illustrative and synthetic; they do not describe a real utility network or corridor.
- The dataset does not redistribute the IEEE case files, FARSITE, PYPOWER/MATPOWER copies, LANDFIRE, NSRDB, or article content unless separately cleared.
- The project must never turn a spatial quality-control result into a claim about operational wildfire prediction, electric-grid reliability, or method performance without separate, evidence-matched validation.
